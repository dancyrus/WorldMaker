# WO-0003-S2: plate motion — remove the permanent-freeze states

CONTEXT. Measured on v0.2.0: plates freeze permanently. At default parameters over 2 Gy at L6, one plate kept ownership overlap 1.000 from 400 My to 2,000 My. Final plate speeds include exact 0.00 deg/My. Dan saw this and reported it. The defect is in the force model in `crates/worldmaker-sim/src/tectonics/step.rs`. The geometry and the Euler-pole motion are correct; do not change them.

RULES. Single-track. Checkpoint commits every 30–45 minutes. Branch: `feat/feel-pass-motion`. If the usage limit nears: commit, push, update checkboxes, stop with a one-line note.

STEPS.

1. Reproduce. Write a headless probe (dev test or example) that runs `run_history` at defaults, 2 Gy, L6, seed 42. For each keyframe pair 200 My apart, compute each plate's ownership overlap: kept cells divided by starting cells. Print each plate's `speed_deg_my` per 200 My. Confirm at least one plate holds overlap ≥ 0.985 across many windows and speed 0.00.
2. Diagnose. Instrument `motion_update()` for one frozen plate. Log per step: `f_coll`, the slab-pull target, the computed `floor` (see the line `let floor = SPEED_MIN * (1.0 - f_coll);`), and `speed_deg_my`. Identify which term holds the speed at zero. Record the finding in the work order.
3. Fix the floor. Requirement: the effective speed floor of every alive plate is ≥ 0.05 deg/My at `tectonic_vigor` = 1.0, including at `f_coll` = 1.0. `COLLISION_DAMP` may stay 1.0 only if the floor change alone meets the requirement; otherwise reduce it. Record the constants you choose.
4. Fix recovery. Requirement: when a plate has `subducting_cells` = 0 and `colliding_cells` = 0, its speed relaxes toward `base_speed_deg_my` at rate `SPEED_RELAX_UP`. Verify with the instrumentation from step 2 that a plate whose collision partner disappears regains speed within 100 My.
5. Fix the suture gap. Facts: `update_pair_timers_and_sutures()` builds pairs from `contact_partner`, and a stopped pair's edges fall below `CLASSIFY_CMYR` (0.4 cm/yr), classify as `F_BND_TRANSFORM`, and stop feeding the pair timer. Requirement: when two plates touch, both sides are continental crust, and the mean `contact_conv_cmyr` is below `SUTURE_SLOW_CMYR`, the pair's `slow_collision_my` still accumulates. After `SUTURE_AFTER_MY` (30 My) the pair sutures. If `contact_partner` is not recorded for sub-threshold contacts, extend `classify_boundaries()` to record it for continent-continent adjacency.
6. Fix the clamp. Requirement: `tectonic_vigor` spreads plate speeds instead of pegging them at `SPEED_MAX` (1.2). Either scale the cap by vigor or raise `SPEED_MAX`. Verify: at vigor 1.73, final speeds are not all equal to the cap.
7. Add the liveliness gates as a fast CI test and as harness metrics:
   7.1 Defaults, 2 Gy, L6, seed 42: no alive plate with ≥ 50 cells keeps ownership overlap ≥ 0.985 across any 300 My window, unless it is inside an active continent-continent collision younger than `SUTURE_AFTER_MY`.
   7.2 Same run: no alive plate holds speed < 0.05 deg/My for more than 200 My contiguous.
   7.3 Echo run at Dan's recorded settings: `seed_from_text("dan")`, `plate_count` 8, `land_fraction` 0.40, `tectonic_vigor` 1.73, `span_my` 2000, L6. Checks 7.1 and 7.2 must pass.
8. Re-run the full acceptance set: Phase 1 harness (age-depth, hypsometry, arcs, determinism), the plategen gates, and the seed-42 2 Gy stability gate. If the stability gate fails, retune per the decision-log entry dated 2026-08-26 (the breakup–suture limit-cycle diagnosis), inside the pinned area bands. Do not weaken any gate.
9. Regenerate GOLDEN_TECTONIC_ELEVATION and GOLDEN_TECTONIC_PLATES once, as the final commit on this branch. This is the second sanctioned golden move of WO-0003; write the decision-log entry in the same style as the M3 entry. Verify the Phase 0 noise golden is unmoved.
10. Push, PR, merge when CI is green, delete the branch. Update the WO-0003 checkboxes. Report to Dan: what froze before, what cannot freeze now, gate results, and the one-line paste for Session 3.

## Session record (2026-08-27, Daniels-MacBook-Air)

**Step 1 — reproduced.** Probe committed as the ignored test
`probe_freeze_report` in crates/worldmaker-sim/tests/liveliness_tests.rs.
At defaults, 2 Gy, L6, seed 42 on the pre-fix code: plates 17, 18, 19 and 20
held speed exactly 0.00 deg/My with per-200-My ownership overlap up to 0.999
from ~1200 My to the end of the run.

**Step 2 — diagnosis (env-gated trace on plate 18, removed after use).**
From t = 1000 My on, every traced step showed `f_coll = 1.000` (131–172
colliding cells vs a saturation of ~52), so the slab-pull target was
`base × (1 + f_sub) × (1 − COLLISION_DAMP × 1) = 0` **and** the floor was
`SPEED_MIN × (1 − 1) = 0`; the clamp held speed at exactly 0 forever. The
colliding classification is contact-based, so it never released, and the
alive count sat at the plate floor (6) where suturing is gated off — the
three ingredients of the permanent freeze. Both terms mattered: the floor
collapse allowed 0.00, the persistent contact + floor-blocked suture made
it permanent.

**Step 3 — floor.** `floor = SPEED_MIN − (SPEED_MIN − SPEED_FLOOR_JAMMED) ×
f_coll` with new `SPEED_FLOOR_JAMMED = 0.05` deg/My: 0.1 unjammed, 0.05 at
`f_coll = 1.0`, meeting the ≥ 0.05 requirement at every vigor.
`COLLISION_DAMP` stays 1.0 — the floor alone meets the requirement (the
clamp enforces it regardless of the zeroed target).

**Step 4 — recovery.** Already structurally present (target relaxes to
`base_speed_deg_my` at `SPEED_RELAX_UP` when `f_sub = f_coll = 0`); verified
by trace: plate 10 dipped to 0.45 deg/My under collision at t = 50 My,
collision cleared, back above its 0.77 base by t = 150 My (< 100 My). The
widened clamp is also visible there (speeds reach 1.22 under slab pull).

**Step 5 — suture gap, two layers.** (a) `contact_partner` IS already
recorded for sub-threshold continent-continent contact, and the pair timer
already accumulates below `SUTURE_SLOW_CMYR` — but the residual creep from
step 3 closes a two-jammed-plate contact at up to ~1.1 cm/yr (2 × 0.05
deg/My), which the old 0.5 cm/yr threshold read as *active* convergence:
timers reset every step and welds ground forever. `SUTURE_SLOW_CMYR` raised
0.5 → 1.2 (above the jam-creep ceiling). (b) Escape flickers: a welded
pair's brief recovery attempts spike the mean above the threshold for a few
steps; the hard reset postponed a measured weld's suture from ~1300 My to
~1610 My. Timers now decay at 2× real time on fast steps instead of
resetting (`SUTURE_DECAY_MULT = 2.0`, the RIFT_DECAY_MULT hysteresis
pattern). (c) Floor gridlock: with the alive count at PLATE_FLOOR and the
supercontinent trigger unreachable, matured pairs could never suture
(engine death, decision log 2026-08-26). `maybe_breakup` now has a gridlock
breaker: at the floor with any timer ≥ SUTURE_AFTER_MY, the
most-continental eligible plate (≥ 32 continental cells, suture age >
100 My) breaks up, opening the one-plate window the suture engine needs —
the breakup↔suture limit cycle without a supercontinent.

**Step 6 — clamp.** `SPEED_MAX` raised 1.2 → 2.0 (above the fastest
possible base draw |N(0.5, 0.15)| × vigor 2.0); setup's base-speed clamp now
shares the step.rs constants. Verified at vigor 1.73: final speeds spread
(0.05–1.6 range observed), none pinned at the cap.

**Step 7 — gates.** Canonical implementation in `tectonics::metrics::
liveliness` (constants LIVELINESS_*), enforced by always-on CI tests
(liveliness_tests.rs: defaults seed 42 + the Dan echo, both 2 Gy L6,

speed ≥ 0.1 deg/My AND contact age ≤ 30 My: catches Easter-style

floor-creep-welded (0.05) plates stay caught by both.
